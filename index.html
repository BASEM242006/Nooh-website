About NOOH

Specializing in Premium Fruit & Vegetable Processing

NOOH is an international food export enterprise dedicated to supplying high-grade fruits and vegetables to global markets. Operating with state-of-the-art IQF (Individually Quick Frozen) technology, we process crops at maximum ripeness to preserve cellular integrity, natural sweetness, and essential nutrients.

The Fresh Frozen Advantage

Freshly Harvested, Immediately Frozen

At NOOH, our products are processed within hours of being picked from the field. Rapid IQF freezing prevents large ice crystal formation, locking in cellular structure, vivid natural color, and up to 98% of natural vitamins.

⏱️

Rapid Harvest-to-Freezer Window

Minimizing time between picking and processing preserves peak sucrose and vitamin levels.

❄️

Zero Ice Crystal Damage

Fluidized bed freezing ensures individual pieces remain loose, non-clumped, and easy to portion.

🥗

Pure & Natural

100% natural fruit and vegetable produce with zero added sugars, artificial preservatives, or colors.
